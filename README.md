### Hi, I'm Harsh Lakkad

I'm a **Structural & Spacecraft Systems Engineer** with 2+ years of hands-on experience in spacecraft structures, stress analysis, **finite element analysis (FEA)** and **re-entry systems**. I hold an M.Sc. in Satellite Technology from Julius-Maximilians-Universität Würzburg and a B.E. in Aeronautical Engineering.

At **ATMOS Space Cargo** I optimised the primary structure of the **Phoenix 2.1** re-entry capsule and analysed the **ICARUS** inflatable ballute. My master's thesis there validated the ballute's fabric and seam design against physical tests. Earlier, I designed structural subsystems and AOCS algorithms for the **AK-EDUSAT** satellite at Aeolus AeroTech.

I'm looking for roles in **launch vehicle structures, spacecraft structural analysis and space systems engineering**.

#### Structural Analysis & Spacecraft Structures Engineer Intern · ATMOS Space Cargo GmbH
`Aug 2025 – Apr 2026` · Lichtenau, Germany · *Phoenix 2.1 re-entry capsule · ICARUS inflatable aeroshell*

- Optimised the Phoenix 2.1 primary load-path bracket in **HyperMesh OptiStruct/Nastran**, reducing wall thickness from 14 to 12 mm and **saving 7.14 kg** against a 500 kg mass budget.
- Ran a **12-load-case nonlinear FEA** of the ICARUS inflatable ballute in **Radioss** (aerodynamic pressure plus re-entry thermal flux), establishing a 0.8 mm design thickness with integrity confirmed across the full trajectory envelope.
- Instrumented **6 structural interfaces** with accelerometers and strain gauges, and correlated sine/random vibration test modal responses to FEA **within ±5 %** per ECSS-E-ST-32.
- Calculated pre-tension and torque specifications for **8 MGSE flange bolted joints**, with positive margins of safety verified.

#### Master's Thesis · ATMOS Space Cargo GmbH × University of Würzburg
`Apr 2026 – Oct 2026` · *ICARUS ballute seam and material design* · [→ repository](https://github.com/harsh-lakkad/ballute-seam-design-reentry)

- Validated orthotropic fabric models against TU Dresden tests (**R² > 0.995**), selected PBO over para-aramid, and recommended a **4-row, 3.5 mm** seam with every comparison within the ECSS 10 % criterion.

#### Space Systems & Structural Analysis Engineer · Julius-Maximilians-Universität Würzburg
`Oct 2024 – May 2025` · Würzburg, Germany · *CARE Orbiter, Mars communication relay* · [→ repository](https://github.com/harsh-lakkad/care-orbiter-mars-relay)

- Modelled the Martian orbital environment (drag, J2–J4 harmonics, solar radiation pressure, third-body effects) in **GMAT** for a 300 × 6,000 km elliptical orbit, informing the propellant budget and mission lifetime.
- Designed a **photovoltaic electrolysis propulsion** system, with propellant sizing and delta-V trades.

#### Spacecraft Systems & AOCS Engineer · Aeolus AeroTech
`Sep 2019 – Aug 2022` · Bangalore, India · *AK-EDUSAT*

- Designed and integrated **7 structural subsystems** (primary structure, separation interface, solar panel brackets, payload panel), from concept to manufacturing release, with ANSYS Mechanical models and full drawings.
- Developed real-time **AOCS stability algorithms in MATLAB/Simulink**, achieving **±1.5° pointing accuracy**.
- Maintained traceability of **230 system-level requirements** in IBM DOORS across PDR, CDR and TRR.
